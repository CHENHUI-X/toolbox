# Overseas Keep-Alive SIM Cards — giffgaff Ban Case + Alternatives (2026)

Session evidence gathered 2026-08 from multiple sources (TT说卡, DuoPlus blog, M.'s Blog, 奶昔论坛, ingstart). Used by the daily deals digest to avoid recommending banned/risky SIM cards.

## ⚠️ giffgaff 2026 mass-ban (do NOT recommend)

- **Scale:** ~120,000+ accounts closed in one cleanup wave. ~72% were mainland-China users (跨境从业者/留学生).
- **Target profile:** long-term roaming outside UK, no local activity — "纯养号" accounts that only received verification codes from China.
- **Hard triggers:** 90+ days with no UK local calls/purchases; long-term login from Chinese IPs; no UK WiFi connection for 6+ months; batch-registered accounts; scripted SMS receive.
- **Consequences:** accounts permanently closed, **balance NOT refunded** (no official refund channel for banned accounts).
- **User context:** user said "Giffgaff 都被封了，你看啥呢" — treat giffgaff as banned and NEVER surface it as a fresh recommendation in the digest. The lesson generalizes: any SIM/vendor recommendation needs a fresh negative-risk search each cycle.

## Porting/refund playbook if user still has a live giffgaff number

1. **Get PAC first** (text `PAC` to 65075, or web: My giffgaff → Thinking of leaving). 30-day validity. PAC moves the NUMBER, not the balance.
2. **Don't rush refunds** — wait until porting succeeds, then request balance refund (14-day cancellation window may apply; otherwise agent review).
3. **Test Wi-Fi Calling before choosing VOXI/CTExcel** — both rely on VoWiFi for receiving SMS in mainland.
4. **Un-ported users: don't panic-transfer** — ban gives ~14-day transition; rushing en masse just gets the next operator banned too.

## Lesson

UK cards are in a risk wave (2026). HK cards (游惠宝 / haha sim) are the current mainstream replacement. Every cycle: search `<card> 封号/风控` before recommending anything.
